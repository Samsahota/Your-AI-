These heads of terms record what we agreed on 17 November 2026, so we can start the Trans Haul pilot on a clear footing. They are not legally binding: the signed Mutual NDA covers confidentiality, and the law of England and Wales applies. A full agreement will follow once the pilot is running, and each of us can take legal advice on it.

# 1. The parties

**Clearpath Insights Ltd** (**Clearpath**) and **YourAI Ltd**, company number 16874666 (**Your AI**).

# 2. What we're doing together

An operations platform for UK hauliers, sold under the Clearpath name. It brings a haulier's own systems together into one dashboard, with figures, actions and daily reports. Trans Haul (THEL (Midlands) Ltd) is the first customer.

# 3. What each of us brings and gets

| | Clearpath | Your AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brings** | The Clearpath Concept, the brand, customer relationships and sales; HR and health and safety content; introductions to each customer's system suppliers | Building, hosting, security and support of the platform; the running costs of hosting |
| **Gets** | A platform under its own brand to sell to hauliers, without paying to build it; a share of every fee | A share of every fee; a sales and industry partner for haulage |
| **Owns** | The Clearpath Concept, brand, content, policies and customer relationships | The platform's software and code |

Each customer owns its own data.

# 4. Customers and their systems

Each customer gives read-only access to its own systems. Where a supplier charges for a licence, user or API access, the customer (or Clearpath, if it agrees with the customer) pays for it. Your AI never changes anything in a customer's systems.

# 5. Prices and the split

| | Trans Haul (founding customer) | Other hauliers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup fee | £3,000: half on starting, half at go-live | £5,000 |
| Setup split (Your AI / Clearpath) | £1,500 / £1,500 | £3,000 / £2,000 |
| Monthly fee | £300, from go-live | £500 |
| Monthly split (Your AI / Clearpath) | £100 running costs to Your AI first, then £100 / £100 | £250 / £250 |

Prices exclude VAT. Add-ons (such as a driver app or customer portal) will be priced and split by agreement.

# 6. Invoicing

**To agree:** either Your AI invoices the customer and pays Clearpath its share, or Clearpath invoices the customer and pays Your AI its share. Either way, each share is paid within 14 days of the customer's payment arriving.

# 7. Working together

- Your AI supplies the platform to UK hauliers through Clearpath while Clearpath is actively selling it. A fair minimum will be set in the full agreement.
- Neither of us approaches the other's customers or contacts to cut the other out.
- If we ever part ways, no customer is left without a system: there will be a handover period, and each customer gets its data back.

# 8. Next steps

1. Sign the NDA.
2. Trans Haul arranges system access, and the pilot starts on its real data.
3. Full agreement drafted and signed before the second customer, or within 3 months, whichever is sooner.
