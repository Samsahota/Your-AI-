# What Clearpath is

Trans Haul uses Clearpath, an online system that brings together information we already hold in our own systems (such as Vigo, Tachomaster, PreDrive, BrightPay and our SharePoint spreadsheets). It shows our managers how the business is doing on safety, service, cost, people and compliance, and what needs action. Clearpath doesn't collect new information about you: it reads what we already hold.

# What information is used

- Your name, employee number, job title, work area and contracted hours.
- Holiday, absence, overtime and working time records.
- For drivers: drivers' hours infringements from Tachomaster, and defects you report on walkaround checks.
- Accident and near-miss reports you're involved in.
- Training, licence and other compliance dates.
- Disciplinary and grievance case records (seen only by the most senior roles).
- If you have a login: your name, work email and sign-in history.

Absence and accident details can include information about your health. We treat it with extra care and limit who can see it.

# Why we use it

We use this information to run the business safely and legally, and to manage our people fairly. That means meeting our legal duties as an employer and a vehicle operator (for example drivers' hours rules and health and safety), managing our contracts with you, and our legitimate interest in managing the business well. We use health information only where needed for our employment and health and safety duties.

# Who can see it

- **Our managers,** according to their role. For example, a Transport Manager sees transport information for their own area. Disciplinary, grievance and payroll detail is seen only by the Director and our consultant.
- **YourAI Ltd,** which runs the system for us under a data processing agreement. It can only use your information to provide the system, and never to train AI.
- **Clearpath Insights Ltd,** our consultant, which supports us with the system.

Your information is stored securely in the UK. Every user signs in with two-factor sign-in, and every change is recorded.

# How long it's kept

We keep information in Clearpath for as long as we keep it in our own records under our retention policy: [for example, the current year plus 6 years for payroll and working time records, and 3 years for accident records, or longer where the law requires]. If we stop using Clearpath, the information is deleted from it.

# Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong. In some cases you can object or ask us to delete or restrict it. To do any of these, contact [name, role, email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), though we'd like the chance to put things right first.
