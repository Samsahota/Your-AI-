This document confirms what we agreed at our meeting on 17 November 2026, our changes to the Mutual NDA, and what we need to continue the build. It is not a legally binding contract: confidentiality is covered by the Mutual NDA, and a full agreement will follow once the Trans Haul pilot is running.

## 1. The parties

**Clearpath Insights Ltd** (Clearpath) and **YourAI Ltd**, company number 16874666 (YourAI).

## 2. What we're doing together

An operations platform for UK hauliers, sold under the Clearpath name. It brings each haulier's own systems together in one place, with live figures, actions and daily reports. Trans Haul (THEL (Midlands) Ltd) is the first customer.

## 3. Who does what

| | Clearpath | YourAI |
|---|---|---|
| **Brings** | The Clearpath Concept and brand; customer relationships and sales; HR and health and safety content; arranging access to each customer's systems | Building, hosting, security and support of the platform; the platform's running costs |
| **Owns** | The Clearpath Concept, brand, content, policies and customer relationships | The platform's software and code |
| **Benefits** | A platform under its own brand to sell to hauliers, with no build cost, and a share of every fee | A share of every fee, and a sales and industry partner in haulage |

Each customer owns its own data.

## 4. Prices and the split

| | Trans Haul (founding customer) | Other hauliers |
|---|---|---|
| Setup fee | £3,000 (half on starting, half at go-live) | £5,000 |
| Setup split (YourAI / Clearpath) | £1,500 / £1,500 | £3,000 / £2,000 |
| Monthly fee | £300, from go-live | £500 |
| Monthly split (YourAI / Clearpath) | £100 running costs to YourAI, then £100 / £100 | £250 / £250 |

Prices exclude VAT. Add-ons, such as a driver app or customer portal, will be priced and split by agreement.

## 5. Invoicing (to confirm)

Either YourAI invoices the customer and pays Clearpath its share, or Clearpath invoices the customer and pays YourAI its share. Either way, each share is paid within 14 days of the customer's payment arriving.

## 6. Working together

- While Clearpath is actively selling, YourAI supplies the platform to UK hauliers through Clearpath. A fair minimum will be set in the full agreement.
- Neither of us approaches the other's customers or contacts to cut the other out.
- If we ever part ways, no customer is left without a system: there will be a handover period, and each customer gets its data back.

## 7. The Mutual NDA

We've returned your NDA with six small changes. Everything else, including clause 6.1, is as you drafted it.

1. **Parties:** YourAI Ltd's registered name and company number added.
2. **Clause 5.3:** software YourAI builds stays YourAI's; Clearpath's brand, content, policies and customer relationships stay Clearpath's.
3. **Clause 5.5:** does not apply to that software. The Clearpath Concept stays protected by clauses 5.2 and 6.1.
4. **Clause 9.2:** the indemnity covers direct losses, up to £25,000, the same for both of us. There is no limit for fraud or deliberate disclosure.
5. **New clause 10.11:** Clearpath arranges read-only access to the systems needed, and any supplier licence or API cost is paid by Clearpath or its Affiliate (for example, Trans Haul).
6. **Signatures:** Pritesh Patel added for YourAI; Samantha's notice email updated to samantha@clearpathinsights.co.uk.

## 8. What we need to continue the build

**System access (read-only).** Clearpath and Trans Haul to arrange this with each supplier. Any cost to switch on access sits with Trans Haul.

- Vigo
- Road Tech: Tachomaster, Falcon Tracking and PreDrive
- SharePoint (Microsoft 365)
- Xero

**Website.** Access to the account that hosts the Clearpath website and domain.

**Documents.**

- Trans Haul's current spreadsheets: holiday, absence, accidents and near misses, training and compliance dates, and the fleet list.
- A copy of the daily report Paul receives each morning.

## 9. Next steps

1. Sign the Mutual NDA.
2. Access and documents as in section 8.
3. YourAI connects each system and checks the figures against Trans Haul's own reports, then takes Paul and Samantha through it.
4. Full agreement signed before the second customer, or within 3 months, whichever is sooner.
