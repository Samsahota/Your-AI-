This agreement sits alongside the Partner Agreement between the same parties. It sets out how both Parties handle customers' personal data in the Clearpath Service. Part A always applies. Part B applies only where Clearpath, rather than Your AI, contracts with and invoices a customer (option B in the Partner Agreement). This is a working draft, not legal advice: have it reviewed before signing.

# Part A: How both Parties handle customer data

A1 **Whose data it is.** Each customer owns its data and is the controller of it. Neither Party uses a customer's data for anything except providing and supporting the Service to that customer.

A2 **Access only as needed.** Each Party limits access to a customer's data to the people who need it to provide or support the Service. Clearpath's staff access it only through their own logins to the platform, at the access level the customer chooses.

A3 **Sensitive data.** Customer data can include health information (absence and accident details), drivers' hours infringements and disciplinary records. Both Parties treat it with extra care. The platform restricts it to the roles each customer allows.

A4 **Security.** Each Party keeps appropriate security in place for the data it handles. This includes two-factor sign-in on every account that can reach customer data, and never sending passwords, keys or customer data by email. Your AI keeps the platform's security measures in place, as described in the customer's data processing agreement.

A5 **No AI training, no selling.** Neither Party uses customer data to train any AI model, sells it, or shares it with anyone not needed to provide the Service.

A6 **Breaches.** Each Party tells the other **within 24 hours** of becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting a customer, gives the details it has, and helps the other so the customer can be told promptly and can notify the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours where required.

A7 **People's rights.** If either Party receives a request from someone about their data in the Service (for example, asking for a copy), it passes it to the customer within 3 working days and helps the customer respond.

A8 **Confidentiality.** Everyone at either Party who can see customer data is bound to keep it confidential.

A9 **Registration.** Each Party keeps its own registration with the Information Commissioner's Office.

A10 **At the end.** When a customer leaves, or this agreement ends, each Party stops using that customer's data. Your AI lets the customer download its data for 30 days and then deletes it (backups on their normal cycle, up to 35 days). Clearpath deletes any copies it holds outside the platform.

# Part B: Where Clearpath is the customer's supplier

B1 **Roles.** Where Clearpath contracts with a customer, Clearpath is the customer's processor and Your AI is Clearpath's **sub-processor**. Clearpath confirms that its contract with the customer allows it to use Your AI and Your AI's service providers listed in B6.

B2 **Instructions.** Your AI processes the customer's data only to provide the Service, on the documented instructions passed on by Clearpath. The Partner Agreement, this agreement and the customer's settings in the platform (such as user access levels) are those instructions. If Your AI believes an instruction breaks data protection law, it will say so.

B3 **The processing.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Purpose** | Providing the Clearpath operations platform to the customer |
| **Duration** | As long as the customer uses the Service, then the period in A10 |
| **People the data is about** | The customer's employees, agency workers and drivers; its platform users; and contacts at its customers and suppliers |
| **Types of data** | As described in the customer's contract: staff and driver records, holiday, absence and overtime, working time, drivers' hours infringements, accident and near-miss reports, compliance dates, payroll totals, disciplinary and grievance records, job, vehicle and customer records, and users' sign-in details |
| **Where data is held** | Supabase, in its London (UK) region |

B4 **Your AI's obligations to Clearpath.** Your AI will:

a. make sure its people are bound to confidentiality;

b. keep the platform's security measures in place;

c. help Clearpath and the customer with people's requests, impact assessments and breaches, as far as the Service allows;

d. at the end, return and delete the data as set out in A10; and

e. give Clearpath the information it reasonably needs to show the customer that this agreement is being met, and allow one audit a year, on 30 days' notice, during working hours, at the requesting party's cost and under confidentiality.

B5 **Breaches.** Your AI tells Clearpath within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach (as in A6), so Clearpath can tell the customer without undue delay.

B6 **Your AI's service providers.** Clearpath authorises Your AI to use these sub-processors, and Your AI gives Clearpath 30 days' notice of any change so Clearpath can tell its customers:

| Provider | What for | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase, Inc. | Database, sign-in, file storage and server functions | Data held in London (UK) |
| [Vercel Inc. or Cloudflare, Inc.: to confirm] | Hosting the web pages (no customer data stored) | Global network |
| Resend, Inc. | Sending the daily and Monday report emails | United States |

Where a provider outside the UK can access data, the transfer is covered by the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (if the provider is certified), or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in the provider's terms. Your AI stays responsible for its providers.

B7 **Matching terms.** Clearpath will not promise a customer anything about data protection beyond what this agreement allows, without Your AI's written agreement.

# Part C: General

C1 **Liability** under this agreement is subject to the Partner Agreement: £25,000 in total for data protection breaches, except where the law does not allow liability to be limited.

C2 This agreement lasts as long as the Partner Agreement and any customer's data remains in the Service, whichever is longer.

C3 It is governed by the law of England and Wales, and may be signed electronically.
