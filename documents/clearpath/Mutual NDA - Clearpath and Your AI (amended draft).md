title: "Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement"

This Agreement is dated 17 November 2026 and protects the confidential information and intellectual property each party shares when discussing a haulage operating platform.

# Parties

1. **Clearpath Insights Ltd** (**Clearpath**).
2. **YourAI Ltd** (company number 16874666) (**Your AI**).

Each a **Party** and together the **Parties**. Each Party may be a Disclosing Party or a Receiving Party.

# Background

- (A) Clearpath has identified a gap in the UK haulage market and developed a concept, business model and operational know-how for an operating platform for hauliers, including an existing operational portal, workflows and associated HR and health and safety policies and processes (the **Clearpath Concept**).
- (B) Your AI has software development capability, technology, methods and tools that may be used to build and operate a platform delivering the Clearpath Concept.
- (C) The Parties wish to meet, starting on 17 November 2026, to discuss a business development plan and strategy for a possible collaboration (the **Purpose**). Each Party will disclose Confidential Information to the other for the Purpose.
- (D) The Parties have agreed to protect that Confidential Information and each other's intellectual property on the terms of this Agreement.

# 1. Definitions

1.1 **Affiliate:** any company that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party. For Clearpath this includes THEL (Midlands) Limited and any company of which a Clearpath director is also a director.

1.2 **Confidential Information:** all information of any nature, in any form (oral, written, visual, electronic or by demonstration), disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party or its Affiliates to the Receiving Party or its Representatives, before, on or after the date of this Agreement, in connection with the Purpose. It includes:

a. for Clearpath: the Clearpath Concept, the identified market gap, business model, pricing and commercial strategy, target customer lists, the existing portal and its screens, workflows, data structures and functionality, HR and health and safety policies, processes and templates, and operational data of Clearpath and its Affiliates;

b. for Your AI: its source code, software architecture, AI models and prompts, tools, development methods, product roadmaps, pricing and technical know-how;

c. for both: business plans, financial information, customer and supplier information, and the existence and content of the Parties' discussions;

d. any notes, analyses, compilations or other material prepared by the Receiving Party that contain or reflect any of the above.

1.3 **Disclosing Party:** the Party (or its Affiliate) disclosing Confidential Information.

1.4 **Receiving Party:** the Party receiving Confidential Information.

1.5 **Intellectual Property Rights:** patents, rights in inventions, copyright and related rights, rights in software, trade marks, business and domain names, goodwill, rights in designs, database rights, rights in confidential information (including know-how and trade secrets), and all similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or not.

1.6 **Background IP:** Intellectual Property Rights owned by or licensed to a Party before the date of this Agreement, or developed independently of the Purpose and without use of the other Party's Confidential Information.

1.7 **Representatives:** a Party's directors, officers, employees, professional advisers and (with the Disclosing Party's prior written consent) contractors, in each case who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

Headings do not affect interpretation. Words such as "including" are illustrative and do not limit the words before them.

# 2. Confidentiality obligations

2.1 The Receiving Party shall:

a. keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential;

b. use it only for the Purpose and for no other purpose, including not to develop, build, train, market or sell any product or service;

c. disclose it only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose, who are told of its confidential nature and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less strict than these;

d. protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care;

e. not copy, reproduce, decompile, reverse engineer or record it, except as strictly needed for the Purpose;

f. not input it into any AI model, large language model or machine-learning tool that is accessible to third parties or that retains or trains on inputs, without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; and

g. notify the Disclosing Party promptly on becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and help to limit its effects.

2.2 The Receiving Party is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that would breach this Agreement if done by the Receiving Party.

# 3. Exclusions

3.1 Clause 2 does not apply to information that the Receiving Party can show by written records:

a. is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement;

b. was lawfully in its possession, free of any duty of confidence, before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

c. is lawfully received from a third party who is not under a duty of confidence; or

d. was independently developed by the Receiving Party without access to or use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

3.2 A combination of features is not excluded just because individual features are public or in the Receiving Party's possession. It is excluded only if the combination itself, and its principle of operation, falls within clause 3.1.

# 4. Compelled disclosure

4.1 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, a court, a regulator or a recognised stock exchange.

4.2 Where lawful, it shall first give the Disclosing Party as much notice as possible, consult on the timing and content, cooperate with any lawful effort to limit disclosure, and disclose only the minimum required.

# 5. Intellectual property

5.1 Each Party keeps all rights in its own Confidential Information and Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers, assigns or licenses any Intellectual Property Rights, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

5.2 Your AI acknowledges that the Clearpath Concept, including the identified market gap, the business model and the commercial approach to the haulage market, originates with Clearpath and is Clearpath's Confidential Information.

5.3 Clearpath acknowledges that Your AI's pre-existing software, code, AI tools, models and development methods are Your AI's Background IP, and that any software, code or platform Your AI writes or builds for the Purpose belongs to Your AI. Clearpath's brand, content, policies and customer relationships remain Clearpath's.

5.4 Any ideas, feedback, designs or materials created jointly by the Parties during discussions under this Agreement (**Discussion Output**) shall be treated as Confidential Information of both Parties. Neither Party may use or exploit Discussion Output, other than for the Purpose, until ownership and use are agreed in a signed definitive agreement.

5.5 Any improvement, adaptation or derivative of a Party's Confidential Information, made by the other Party, belongs to the Party whose Confidential Information it derives from. The Party making it shall assign it on request and at its own cost. This clause does not apply to Your AI's software, code or platform described in clause 5.3.

5.6 Confidential Information is provided "as is". No warranty is given as to its accuracy or completeness, or that its use will not infringe third-party rights.

5.7 Neither Party may file any patent, trade mark, domain name or other registration that contains, is based on or would conflict with the other Party's Confidential Information.

# 6. Non-circumvention and non-solicitation

These restrictions last from the date of this Agreement until 24 months after the end of discussions under clause 7.2 (the **Restricted Period**).

6.1 During the Restricted Period, Your AI shall not, directly or indirectly, alone or with any other person, develop, build, market, license or operate any product or service for the UK haulage or road transport sector that is based on, derived from or substantially similar to the Clearpath Concept, except under a signed definitive agreement with Clearpath.

6.2 During the Restricted Period, Clearpath shall not use Your AI's Confidential Information (including its architecture, code or technical methods) to build, or to brief any third party to build, a platform delivering the Clearpath Concept.

6.3 Clause 6.2 does not stop Clearpath from developing the Clearpath Concept itself or with another provider, provided it does not use Your AI's Confidential Information.

6.4 During the Restricted Period, neither Party shall, without the other's prior written consent, solicit, approach or deal with any customer, prospective customer, supplier or business contact introduced by the other Party in connection with the Purpose, for a product or service competing with the Clearpath Concept.

6.5 During the Restricted Period, neither Party shall solicit or entice away any employee or contractor of the other Party with whom it had contact in connection with the Purpose. General recruitment advertising not aimed at those people is allowed.

6.6 Each Party confirms these restrictions are reasonable and no wider than needed to protect its legitimate business interests. If any restriction is found unenforceable but would be valid if reduced in scope or duration, it applies with the minimum modification needed to make it valid.

# 7. Term

7.1 This Agreement starts on the date at the top and applies to Confidential Information disclosed before that date in connection with the Purpose.

7.2 Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice (email is enough). Discussions also end automatically if no definitive agreement is signed within 12 months of the date of this Agreement.

7.3 The obligations in clauses 2 to 5 continue for 5 years after discussions end. For trade secrets and source code, they continue for as long as the information remains confidential.

7.4 Clause 6 continues for the Restricted Period. Clauses 8 to 10 survive indefinitely.

# 8. Return and destruction

8.1 Within 10 working days of the Disclosing Party's written request, or of discussions ending, the Receiving Party shall return or securely destroy all Confidential Information and confirm in writing, signed by a director, that it has done so.

8.2 The Receiving Party may keep one archive copy where required by law or regulation, or held in routine IT back-ups that cannot reasonably be deleted. Any copy kept remains subject to this Agreement.

# 9. Remedies

9.1 Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach of this Agreement. The Disclosing Party is entitled to seek an injunction, specific performance or other equitable relief without proof of special damage, in addition to any other remedy.

9.2 The Party in breach shall indemnify the other against reasonable direct losses, costs and legal fees arising from any breach of clauses 2, 5 or 6, up to a total of £25,000. This limit does not apply to fraud or to deliberate disclosure of Confidential Information.

# 10. General

10.1 **No obligation to proceed:** this Agreement does not oblige either Party to disclose any information, to continue discussions or to enter into any further agreement. It creates no partnership, joint venture or agency.

10.2 **No public announcement:** neither Party shall announce or publicise the discussions or name the other Party without its prior written consent.

10.3 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and replaces any earlier arrangement on confidentiality.

10.4 **Variation and waiver:** changes must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Not exercising a right is not a waiver of it.

10.5 **Assignment:** neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other's prior written consent.

10.6 **Severance:** if any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the rest of this Agreement continues in effect.

10.7 **Third parties:** the Affiliates of each Party may enforce clauses 2 to 9 in respect of their own Confidential Information under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999. Otherwise, no third party has rights under this Agreement, and the Parties may vary it without any Affiliate's consent.

10.8 **Notices:** notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by first-class post to the registered office, or by email to the address in the signature block.

10.9 **Counterparts and e-signature:** this Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

10.10 **Governing law and jurisdiction:** this Agreement and any dispute arising from it, including non-contractual disputes, are governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, except that either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court.

10.11 **Access to systems:** where the Purpose needs access to systems used by Clearpath or its Affiliates (for example, Trans Haul's operating, telematics, finance and HR systems), Clearpath shall arrange read-only access for Your AI and, where a supplier charges for a licence, user or API access, Clearpath or its Affiliate shall buy it. Your AI shall use that access only for the Purpose.
