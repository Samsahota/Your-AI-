**1. Parties.** "Your AI Ltd" becomes **"YourAI Ltd (company number 16874666)"**: the company's registered name and number.

**2. Clause 5.3 (ownership, both ways).** Your wording kept, adding: *"and that any software, code or platform Your AI writes or builds for the Purpose belongs to Your AI. Clearpath's brand, content, policies and customer relationships remain Clearpath's."*
Why: each of us keeps what we bring.

**3. Clause 5.5.** Your wording kept, adding: *"This clause does not apply to Your AI's software, code or platform described in clause 5.3."*
Why: so the platform isn't caught by 5.5. The Clearpath Concept stays protected by 5.2 and 6.1.

Why: the same for both of us, and insurable.
