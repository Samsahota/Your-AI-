# Parties and background

This agreement is dated x and is between:

1. **Your AI Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales (company number x), whose registered office is at x ("**Your AI**"); and
2. **THEL (Midlands) Ltd**, trading as Trans Haul, a company registered in England and Wales (company number x), whose registered office is at x, Dunstable ("**Trans Haul**").

**Background**

A. Your AI has developed a cloud-based operator platform for hauliers (the "**Platform**"). It brings together information from a haulier's existing systems, highlights what needs attention, and helps the team act on it.

B. The Platform is not a transport management system. It does not route vehicles, raise invoices, book jobs or calculate drivers' hours. Those remain in the haulier's existing systems.

C. Trans Haul wishes to be the first haulier to use the Platform, through a pilot configured to its operation, on the terms below.

D. Your AI intends to offer the Platform to other hauliers, including through Clearpath under a separate partner agreement. Trans Haul's founding-customer terms are set out in clause 8.

# 1. Definitions

- **Acceptance** — Trans Haul confirming, under clause 4, that a milestone meets its acceptance criteria.
- **Add-on Modules** — the optional modules listed in Schedule 1, Part B, that Trans Haul chooses to take.
- **Core Modules** — the modules listed in Schedule 1, Part A.
- **Customer Data** — all data that Trans Haul, its staff, drivers or customers put into the Platform, or that the Platform reads from Trans Haul's systems.
- **Go-Live** — the date Trans Haul starts using the Platform in its daily operation, after Acceptance of the final milestone.
- **Pass-through Costs** — third-party software and service costs incurred only to run Trans Haul's instance (for example hosting, email delivery and integration tools), listed in Schedule 2.
- **Pilot** — the discovery, configuration, build, testing and first x months of live use described in Schedule 1.
- **Source Systems** — Trans Haul's existing systems the Platform reads from, including Vigo, Tachomaster, its accounting system and SharePoint.

# 2. The pilot

2.1 Your AI will configure and build the Platform for Trans Haul as set out in Schedule 1, and provide it as a hosted service from Go-Live.

2.2 The Platform reads the Source Systems through read-only connections, scheduled reports or file exports. It does not write back to them. Trans Haul's existing systems remain its systems of record.

2.3 Scope will be confirmed in writing after the discovery day (clause 4.1). Until then, Schedule 1 is the working scope.

# 3. Out of scope and changes

3.1 The following are not part of the Pilot: vehicle routing, job booking and planning in place of the TMS, invoicing, payroll, and the calculation or enforcement of drivers' hours.

3.2 Either party may request a change to the scope in writing. Your AI will reply within x working days with the effect on price and timetable. No change takes effect until both parties agree it in writing.

3.3 Changes are charged at x per day, or at a fixed price agreed for that change.
