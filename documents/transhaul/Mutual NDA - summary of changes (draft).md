---
title: "Mutual NDA: summary of our proposed changes"
subtitle: "Clearpath Insights Ltd and YourAI Ltd · 18 November 2026 · draft for discussion"
---

The NDA stays mutual, and everything not listed below is unchanged from Clearpath's draft of 17 November 2026. The changes make ownership even-handed (each Party keeps what it brings), limit the indemnity, set out who provides access to the systems, and add Pritesh as a signatory.

| Clause | Clearpath's draft | Proposed change | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background (B) | Your AI "has… tools that may be used to build and operate a platform delivering the Clearpath Concept" | Your AI "has developed software… including its own haulage operating platform, that may be used to deliver services to hauliers, including services delivering the Clearpath Concept" | Records that the platform is Your AI's own, as the Clearpath Concept is Clearpath's own |
| Background (D) | Protect each other's intellectual property | Adds: "Each Party keeps what it brings" | States the principle both ways |
| 1.6 Background IP | General definition | Adds examples for each Party: Clearpath's concept, brand, policies, portal and customer relationships; Your AI's software, platform, connectors and demonstration portal | Makes each side's existing property clear |
| 1.7, 2.1(f), 2.2, new 2.3 | No AI tools that keep or train on inputs; contractors need consent | Either Party may use business hosting, cloud, email and AI services bound to keep data confidential and not train on it | Both Parties use cloud and AI tools day to day; the data stays protected |
| New 2.4 | Not covered | Each Party pays for its own system licences. Clearpath procures (or, for an Affiliate it doesn't control, uses all reasonable endeavours to procure) that it and its Affiliates (including THEL) buy or enable any API or integration module the supplier charges for, authorise read-only access for Your AI, and share access details securely. Your AI uses access read-only and only for the Purpose | The platform depends on access to these systems, and the cost of each system sits with the business that uses it |
| 5.2 | Your AI acknowledges the Clearpath Concept originates with Clearpath | Kept, limited to what Clearpath disclosed; general sector, software and integration knowledge excluded | Mutual with 5.3 |
| 5.3 | Clearpath acknowledges Your AI's pre-existing software, code, AI tools, models and methods | Adds the platform, connectors, designs and the demonstration portal built before the Agreement | Mutual with 5.2 |
| 5.4 | Joint Discussion Output can't be used until a deal is signed | Adds: this does not limit either Party's use of its own Background IP | Stops the clause catching what each side already owns |
| 5.5 and new 5.8 | Any derivative of a Party's Confidential Information belongs to that Party | Kept, but 5.8 confirms both ways: Your AI's platform stays Your AI's whatever content it carries; Clearpath's concept, brand and content stay Clearpath's whatever platform they're in; customer data stays the customer's | Each side keeps what it brings |
| 9.2 | Uncapped indemnity for reasonable losses, costs and legal fees | Direct losses only, capped at £25,000 in total; no cap for fraud or deliberate disclosure; no liability for indirect loss or loss of profit | A responsible, insurable limit, the same for both Parties |
| Signatures | Samantha's notice email is her Waitrose address; Your AI signs once | Samantha's Clearpath address; Pritesh Patel (Co-founder, pritesh@yourailtd.co.uk) added as a Your AI signatory | Correct notice address; Pritesh is part of Your AI's side |

Unchanged: confidentiality obligations (2.1(a) to (e) and (g)), exclusions (3), compelled disclosure (4), 5.1, 5.6, 5.7, non-circumvention and non-solicitation (6.1 to 6.6), term (7), return and destruction (8), injunctions (9.1) and the general clauses (10).
