# Trans Haul discovery session pack

16 November 2026 · Sam Sahota, Your AI Ltd

The aim of the session is to leave with one chosen package, one chosen dashboard layout, a list of data sources with who gives access, and a named decision-maker. Everything here is a starting point to react to; nothing is built until Paul picks.

What stays out of every package: booking jobs, routing, invoicing and calculating drivers' hours. Those stay in Vigo, Xero and Tachomaster.

For Trans Haul, the likely fit is **2 or 3**: Paul wants the manual workarounds pulled together (package 2), and walkarounds are still on paper (the driver app in package 3).

## Dashboard layout options

Five ways to lay out the home screen. Each role can have a different one; most hauliers will want a different home page for the director, the ops manager and the transport manager. Wireframes of all five are in the visual sheet.

| Layout | What you see first | Suits | Strength | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Five questions | Yesterday, costs, PODs and billing, cash, today, in that order, top to bottom | Director's morning read | Tells the whole story in reading order | Takes a scroll |
| B. Exceptions first | A short to-do list of what needs action now, ranked; numbers underneath | Operations manager | Nothing to interpret: just act | Less sense of the overall picture |
| C. One-screen scorecard | 8–10 tiles, each green, amber or red against a target; no scrolling | Director on the move; a TV in the office | Read in 10 seconds; prints on one page | Less detail; needs targets agreed |
| D. Today board | Today's jobs in columns (not started, en route, on site, done), cover gaps at the top | Planners and ops; office wallboard | Live view of the day | Says nothing about yesterday or money |
| E. Compliance first | A grid of vehicles and drivers, coloured by what is due or overdue; defects at the top | Transport manager | Nothing slips through | Operational only, no commercial view |

A suggested starting point for Trans Haul: **director A or C, ops manager B, transport manager E**, with D available to everyone as a tab. Ask Paul which one he would open first thing, and what he would delete.

## Pricing structure

Price it as an operator platform: clearly cheaper than a TMS, simple to understand, and with connecting each client's systems kept as your own separate service. Three charges, nothing else.

1. **Setup (one-off):** configuring the package for that haulier, their rate card, users and training.
2. **Subscription (monthly, per company):** the package they use. Cancel with notice after the first term.
3. **Connections (one-off per system, plus a small monthly fee):** your job per client, once they give API access or reports. The monthly fee covers monitoring the feed and fixing it when a supplier changes something.

The figures below are **suggested starting ranges to test with Paul, not quotes**. Confirm them after scoping, and check them against what he pays for Vigo and Tachomaster (question 39).

| Package | Setup (one-off) | Subscription (per month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Morning Dashboard | £1,500–£2,500 | £200–£350 |
| 2. Operator Core | £4,000–£7,000 | £450–£750 |
| 3. Core + Driver App | add £1,500–£2,500 | add £150–£250 |
| 4. Full Platform | add £1,500–£2,500 | add £150–£250 |

| Connection type | One-off | Monthly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System with an API (Vigo, Xero, telematics) | £750–£1,500 each | £50–£75 each |
| Scheduled email report or file export (Tachomaster reports, Bibby statement) | £500–£1,000 each | £50–£75 each |
| AI reading of emailed supplier invoices | £1,500–£2,500 | £75–£125 |

Alternative for resale: charge **per vehicle** instead, for example £25–£40 per vehicle a month for Operator Core. For Trans Haul's 18 vehicles that is roughly £450–£720 a month. It scales naturally with bigger and smaller hauliers.

**Founding-customer offer for Trans Haul:** 50% off setup, the monthly fee fixed for 12 months, and a referral fee for each haulier they bring in. Ask for 50% up front, as discussed.

**Your running costs:** hosting, database and email are roughly £50–£60 a month per client at Trans Haul's size, plus a few pounds of AI usage if invoices are read automatically. Pass these through at cost, open-book, or include them in the subscription; for resale, including them is simpler.
