This is a short, plain-English agreement for how Clearpath and Your AI work together to sell and run the Clearpath platform for UK hauliers. It is a working draft, not legal advice. Each Party should have a solicitor look over it before signing.

# Parties

1. **Clearpath Insights Ltd**, company number [ ], registered office [ ] (**Clearpath**).
2. **Your AI Ltd**, company number [ ], registered office [ ] (**Your AI**).

Each a **Party**, together the **Parties**.

# 1. What this agreement is for

1.1 Clearpath has the concept, the market access and the customer relationships. Your AI has built, and runs, the software platform that delivers it. Together the Parties offer an operations platform to UK hauliers under the Clearpath name (the **Service**).

1.2 Trans Haul (THEL (Midlands) Ltd) is the first customer and founding pilot.

1.3 The mutual non-disclosure agreement between the Parties (the **NDA**) continues to apply. If this agreement and the NDA conflict, this agreement applies.

# 2. Who does what

2.1 **Clearpath:**

a. owns and markets the Clearpath name and brand;

b. finds and sells to customers, runs demonstrations, and manages the customer relationship;

c. provides its HR and health and safety policies, processes and templates for the policy library, and confirms it is licensed to share them with customers;

d. is each customer's first point of contact, and passes technical questions to Your AI; and

e. obtains each customer's agreement to buy or enable access to its own systems (clause 6).

2.2 **Your AI:**

a. builds, hosts, secures, maintains and supports the platform;

b. sets up each customer, including connecting their systems and setting up their users;

c. provides support and fixes as set out in Schedule 2;

d. joins one technical call per prospective customer at no charge, with further calls charged as set out in Schedule 1; and

e. keeps professional indemnity and cyber insurance in place while the Service runs.

2.3 Neither Party may make promises to a customer on the other's behalf, such as new features, prices or dates, without the other's written agreement.

# 3. Who owns what

3.1 **Each Party keeps what it brings.**

a. Clearpath owns its concept, its name and brand, its policies, processes and templates, its content, and its customer relationships.

b. Your AI owns the platform, its software and code, its connectors to other systems, its designs, and any improvements to them, including those made while delivering the Service.

c. Each customer owns its own data.

3.2 Your AI gives Clearpath the right to market, sell and demonstrate the platform as the Service while this agreement runs.

3.3 Clearpath gives Your AI the right to use the Clearpath name, brand and content in and around the platform, only to deliver the Service, while this agreement runs.

3.4 Your AI may reuse its connectors and platform for other customers of the Service. That is what makes each new haulier quicker and cheaper to set up.

# 4. Working together

4.1 **Exclusivity.** While Clearpath is selling actively, Your AI supplies the platform to UK hauliers only through Clearpath. If Clearpath brings in fewer than [ ] new customers in any 12 months, either Party may end this exclusivity by giving written notice.

4.2 **Prices** are set out in Schedule 1. Either Party may propose a change, and changes apply once both agree in writing. Prices to customers rise once a year, in line with inflation and suppliers' costs.

4.3 **Customer terms.** Each customer signs short customer terms and a data processing agreement before go-live. The minimum term is 12 months from go-live, then rolling monthly with 3 months' notice.

# 5. Money

5.1 **The split.** Fees from each customer are shared as set out in Schedule 1.

5.2 **Invoicing.** [Option A, proposed: Your AI invoices each customer for the setup and monthly fees. Clearpath invoices Your AI for its share once the customer has paid, and Your AI pays within 14 days of receiving Clearpath's invoice.] [Option B: Clearpath invoices each customer and pays Your AI its share within 14 days of the customer paying.] *The Parties to choose one before signing.*

5.3 **Paid when paid.** Neither Party owes the other a share of money a customer has not paid. If a customer pays only part of an invoice, it is shared in proportion.

5.4 **Statement.** The invoicing Party sends a monthly statement showing what each customer was invoiced and paid, and each Party's share.

5.5 **Setup timing.** Setup fees are invoiced 50% on order and 50% on go-live. Monthly fees start from go-live.

5.6 **Running costs.**

a. For Trans Haul, £100 of each monthly fee covers the platform's running costs and goes to Your AI before the split. If running costs for Trans Haul go above £100 a month, the excess also comes off before the split.

b. For every other customer, Your AI pays the running costs out of its share.

# 6. Customers' systems and access

6.1 Each customer is responsible for its own licences and subscriptions to its own systems (for example its transport management, tachograph, tracking, accounts, payroll and document systems).

6.2 Where a supplier charges for an API, an integration module or an extra user so that the platform can read the customer's data, the customer pays for it. Clearpath shall make this clear to each customer before it signs.

6.3 Your AI reads customer systems read-only and never changes data in them. Access details are shared securely, never by email.

6.4 Your AI is not responsible for a supplier's own system, prices or changes. If a supplier changes its system significantly, Your AI quotes for the rework.

# 7. Data protection

7.1 Each customer is the data controller of its data. Your AI processes it on the customer's behalf under a data processing agreement with that customer.

7.2 Clearpath may see a customer's data only through its own login to the platform, with the customer's agreement, and only for supporting that customer.

7.3 Each Party keeps its own registration with the Information Commissioner's Office.

# 8. Liability

8.1 Each Party's total liability to the other under this agreement in any 12 months is limited to the amount it received under this agreement in the previous 12 months.

8.2 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit.

8.3 Nothing in this agreement limits liability for fraud, or for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or anything else that cannot legally be limited.

# 9. Term and ending

9.1 This agreement starts on the date it is signed. It runs for 12 months, then continues until either Party gives 3 months' written notice.

9.2 Either Party may end it straight away by written notice if the other:

a. commits a serious breach that is not put right within 30 days of being told about it; or

b. becomes insolvent.

9.3 **If this agreement ends:**

a. existing customers keep their service for at least 6 months, or until the end of their current term if later, so no customer is left without a system;

b. Clearpath's share of the monthly fees continues for as long as each customer it introduced stays on the Service;

c. Your AI keeps its platform, code and connectors. Clearpath keeps its name, brand, content and customer relationships; and

d. each Party stops using the other's name, brand and content, except as needed for clause 9.3(a).

# 10. General

10.1 **Disputes.** The Parties will first try to settle any disagreement by talking, director to director. If that fails within 30 days, they will try mediation before going to court.

10.2 **Relationship.** This agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture or employment. Neither Party may bind the other.

10.3 **Changes** to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent.

10.5 **Signing.** This agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.

10.6 **Law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
