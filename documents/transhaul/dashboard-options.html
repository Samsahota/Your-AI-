The same data, arranged five ways. Each role can have a different one. A sensible start: director on A or C, operations on B, transport on E.

Five questions

  • Are we making money on the work?
  • Where is margin leaking?
  • What's delivered but not billed?
  • Are we compliant and safe?
  • Is cash OK?
