# 1. The Service

1.1 The Service brings together information from Trans Haul's own systems, shows it as figures against Trans Haul's targets, and highlights what needs action.

1.2 The Service is not a transport management system. It does not plan routes, book jobs, raise customer invoices or calculate drivers' hours. Those stay in Trans Haul's own systems.

1.3 The Service reads Trans Haul's systems **read-only**. It never changes data in them.

# 2. Setup and go-live

2.1 Your AI sets up the Service, connects each system once Trans Haul has given access, and sets up Trans Haul's users.

2.2 Before each system's figures are switched on, Your AI checks them against Trans Haul's own reports. Trans Haul will tell Your AI promptly if a figure looks wrong.

2.3 **Go-live** is the date both sides confirm in writing (email is enough) that the Service is in use, with at least Vigo connected and its figures checked. Trans Haul will not unreasonably delay confirming it.

2.4 Delays caused by a supplier, or by access not yet given, do not count against Your AI.

# 3. What Trans Haul provides

3.1 Trans Haul is responsible, at its own cost, for its licences and subscriptions to its own systems. Where a supplier charges for an API, an integration module or an extra user so that the Service can read Trans Haul's data, Trans Haul pays for it.

3.2 Trans Haul will:

a. authorise each supplier in writing to give Your AI read-only access to its data;

b. share access details securely, using the method Your AI provides, and never by email;

c. name a contact for setup and questions; and

d. keep its own systems' data accurate, since the figures are only as accurate as the data they come from.

# 4. Users and security

4.1 Every user signs in with a password and a code from an authenticator app (two-factor sign-in). Users must not share logins.

4.2 Trans Haul decides who has access and at what level. Its director can invite, change and suspend users, and should suspend leavers on the day they leave.

4.3 Trans Haul is responsible for what its users do in the Service.

4.4 Users must not try to break the Service's security, copy or resell it, or use it unlawfully.

# 5. The operator's own duties

5.1 The Service supports Trans Haul's management. It does not replace any of Trans Haul's legal duties, including:

- drivers' hours and tachograph rules;
- the undertakings on its operator licence;
- vehicle maintenance, walkaround checks and defect reporting;
- health and safety law; and
- employment law.

5.2 Trans Haul, its directors and its transport manager remain responsible for those duties to the DVSA, the Traffic Commissioner and any other authority, whatever the Service shows or misses.

# 6. Support and availability

6.1 Included in the monthly fee:

- hosting, backups, monitoring and security updates;
- keeping connections running, including small changes by suppliers;
- bug fixes;
- adding, changing and removing users; and
- email support on working days, with a reply within one working day.

6.2 Not included, and quoted first:

- new features, reports or modules;
- new connections;
- rework after a supplier changes its system significantly; and
- training beyond go-live.

6.3 Your AI aims to keep the Service available at all times, apart from planned maintenance, which it schedules outside 07:00 to 19:00 on working days where it can. The Service depends on internet and supplier services outside Your AI's control.

# 7. Fees and payment

7.1 Fees are set out in the Order details. Invoices are due within 14 days.

7.2 Late payments carry interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

7.3 If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, Your AI may suspend the Service after giving 7 days' written notice. It will restore it once the invoice is paid.

7.4 Fees may rise once a year, in line with inflation and suppliers' costs, with 30 days' written notice.

# 8. Trans Haul's data

8.1 Trans Haul owns its data. Your AI processes it only to provide the Service, under the Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of these terms.

8.2 When these terms end, Trans Haul can download its data in a standard format for 30 days. Your AI then deletes it, apart from backups, which are deleted on their normal cycle.

# 9. Ownership

9.1 Your AI owns the platform, its software and its connectors. Clearpath owns its name, brand and policy content.

9.2 While these terms run, Trans Haul may use the Service, including the Clearpath content in it, for its own business.

# 10. Confidentiality

Each side keeps the other's confidential information confidential, and uses it only for the Service. This continues for 5 years after these terms end.

# 11. Clearpath

11.1 Clearpath Insights Ltd is Your AI's partner. It introduced the Service, provides the policy content, and is Trans Haul's first point of contact for the Service.

11.2 Trans Haul agrees that Clearpath may have its own login to support Trans Haul, with the access level Trans Haul chooses. Clearpath keeps Trans Haul's data confidential and secure, and uses it only for supporting Trans Haul.

# 12. Liability

12.1 Each side's total liability to the other under these terms in any 12 months is limited to the greater of £5,000 and the fees paid in the previous 12 months.

12.2 Neither side is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill.

12.3 Your AI is not liable for:

- errors in Trans Haul's own systems or data;
- a supplier's system, prices or changes; or
- anything that is part of Trans Haul's legal duties under clause 5.

12.4 If data is lost through Your AI's fault, Your AI's responsibility is to restore it from its most recent backup.

12.5 Nothing limits liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for anything else that cannot legally be limited.

# 13. Term and ending

13.1 These terms start when signed, and run for the minimum term in the Order details, then continue until either side gives 3 months' written notice.

13.2 Either side may end these terms straight away by written notice if the other:

- commits a serious breach that is not put right within 30 days of being told about it; or
- becomes insolvent.

13.3 If Trans Haul ends these terms before go-live for its own convenience, the setup fee already paid is not refunded.

# 14. General

14.1 Neither side is responsible for delays caused by events outside its reasonable control.

14.2 Changes to these terms must be in writing and agreed by both sides.

14.3 These terms, the Order details and the Data Processing Agreement are the whole agreement for the Service.

14.4 They may be signed electronically. They are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
